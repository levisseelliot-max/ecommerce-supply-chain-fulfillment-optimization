# **Problem Definition: Root Cause Decomposition**

Root cause analysis confirms network friction is driven by middle and last-mile transit rather than merchant fulfillment speeds:

* **Intra-State Corridor (35.8% volume):** Operates efficiently with **4.72 transit days** (7.88 total lead time) and minimal risk.  
* **Inter-State Corridor (64.2% volume):** Experiences severe bottlenecks at **11.69 transit days (+148%)**, generating **$185,735 (75.4%)** of total margin loss.  
* **Regional Concentration:** Over **70% of shipments** originate in São Paulo, creating extreme long-haul delays to the Northeast (e.g., **26.4 days to Roraima**).

# **Strategic Pillars**

* **Pillar 1: Forward-Deployed 3PL Hubs (Recife & Salvador):** Pre-position top 20% SKUs in Northeastern hubs. **Impact: Reduces transit from 18-26 days to \<4.5 days, recovering $68,000 in penalties.**  
* **Pillar 2: Dynamic Multi-Carrier Allocation & Routing API:** Dynamically allocate freight based on trailing OTIF and enforce SLA clawbacks. **Impact: Mitigates $48,350 in customer churn costs.**  
* **Pillar 3: High-Risk Key Account Remediation Program:** Enforce 48h dispatch triggers with buy-box perks for ≥95% OTIF and throttling for \<90% OTIF. **Impact: Recovers $35,000 in merchant-driven leakage.**
